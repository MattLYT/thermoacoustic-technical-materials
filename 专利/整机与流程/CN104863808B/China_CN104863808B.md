# CN104863808B - Multistage traveling wave thermoacoustic engine system for cascade utilization of high-temperature flue gas waste heat - Google Patents

## Abstract

一种梯级利用高温烟气余热的多级行波热声发动机系统，其为N级热声发动机单元通过谐振管首尾相连构成的环路结构，N＝3～10正整数；每一热声发动机单元由依次相连的直流抑制器、主冷却器、回热器、加热器、高温端层流化元件、热缓冲管、室温端层流化元件、次冷却器和谐振管组成；系统工作时，高温烟气余热依次通过每一热声发动机单元的加热器，与之换热从而使加热器产生高温，发动机回热器处形成温度梯度，在该温度梯度下热能转换成声功，声功沿着温度梯度的正方向传播并依次经过多次放大，如此循环工作；本发明无机械运动部件，结构紧凑，能够梯级高效利用烟气余热产生声功，节能减排。

A multi-stage traveling wave thermoacoustic engine system utilizing high-temperature flue gas waste heat in stages, which is a loop structure composed of N-stage thermoacoustic engine units connected end to end through resonant tubes, N = 3 to 10 positive integers; each thermoacoustic engine The unit is composed of DC suppressor, main cooler, regenerator, heater, high temperature end laminar fluidization element, thermal buffer tube, room temperature end laminar fluidization element, secondary cooler and resonance tube connected in sequence; when the system is working, The waste heat of the high-temperature flue gas passes through the heaters of each thermoacoustic engine unit in turn, exchanging heat with them so that the heaters generate high temperature, and a temperature gradient is formed at the engine regenerator. It propagates in the positive direction of the temperature gradient and undergoes multiple amplifications in turn, so that it works cyclically; the invention has no mechanical moving parts, has a compact structure, and can efficiently use the waste heat of the flue gas to generate sound power in steps, saving energy and reducing emissions.

背景技术 Background technique

能源是经济发展和社会进步的重要基础，节能减排是我国更好进行现代化建设、发展市场经济的关键问题。余热利用作为节能减排中的重要部分，具有巨大的发展潜力。余热属于二次能源，在我国资源丰富，广泛存在电站锅炉及工业设备中，其中高温烟气余热总量约占余热总资源的50％。锅炉烟气热损失、汽车尾气余热是各项热损失中很大的一项，不仅浪费了大量潜在的能源，而且过高的排烟温度严重影响锅炉经济运行，或者产生城市热岛效应。如果能有效利用这些余热，则可节约大量能源，减少大气污染，且降低企业生产成本，很好地实现节能减排。Energy is an important basis for economic development and social progress, and energy conservation and emission reduction is a key issue for my country to better carry out modernization and develop a market economy. Waste heat utilization, as an important part of energy conservation and emission reduction, has huge development potential. Waste heat belongs to secondary energy. It is rich in resources in my country and widely exists in power plant boilers and industrial equipment. The total amount of high-temperature flue gas waste heat accounts for about 50% of the total waste heat resources. Boiler flue gas heat loss and automobile exhaust waste heat are the largest items of heat loss, not only wasting a lot of potential energy, but also excessively high exhaust gas temperature seriously affects the economic operation of boilers, or creates urban heat island effect. If the waste heat can be effectively utilized, a large amount of energy can be saved, air pollution can be reduced, production costs can be lowered, and energy conservation and emission reduction can be well realized.

热声发动机是一种利用管件和换热器在其内部获得合适的声场，并通过工作介质和回热器之间的相互作用将热能转化为声能的装置，具有无机械运动部件、可靠性高、寿命长等优点。根据热声转换的声场特性，热声发动机分为行波热声发动机和驻波热声发动机。行波热声发动机基于可逆的热声斯特林循环，相较于基于不可逆循环的驻波热声发动机而言具有潜在的高效率，是热声热机研究和发展的重要方向。A thermoacoustic engine is a device that uses pipes and heat exchangers to obtain a suitable sound field inside it, and converts heat energy into sound energy through the interaction between the working medium and the regenerator. It has no mechanical moving parts, reliability High, long life and other advantages. According to the sound field characteristics of thermoacoustic conversion, thermoacoustic engines are divided into traveling wave thermoacoustic engines and standing wave thermoacoustic engines. The traveling wave thermoacoustic engine is based on the reversible thermoacoustic Stirling cycle. Compared with the standing wave thermoacoustic engine based on the irreversible cycle, the traveling wave thermoacoustic engine has potential high efficiency, and it is an important direction for the research and development of thermoacoustic heat engine.

The engine is mainly composed of a traveling wave loop and a resonance tube. From the perspective of sound field properties, this is a high-efficiency traveling/standing wave hybrid thermoacoustic engine. The end of the standing wave resonance tube of the thermoacoustic engine introduces a local traveling wave circuit, which greatly improves the performance. However, a large part of the acoustic power of the thermoacoustic engine is dissipated in the standing wave resonant tube, and due to the large size of the resonant tube, the system power density is low, which restricts its further application.

图2为De Block等人的WO2010107308A1公布的一种多级行波热声发动机系统。该多级行波热声发动机系统由多个相同的行波热声发动机组成，每个行波热声发动机通过谐振管相连构成一个环路。该系统能够有效地回收耗散在谐振管中的声功，且避免了谐振管尺寸过大的问题，相对于图1所述的热声发动机系统结构更为紧凑，潜在效果更高。但是，在该热声发动机中未加入热缓冲管结构，造成冷热气体混合损失，导致热量大量损耗在谐振管中，同时在环路系统中未安装直流器，所以这种结构也未能取得较好的效果。特别是，该系统不适合利用烟气排热这种热源温度逐渐降低的余热。Fig. 2 is a multi-stage traveling wave thermoacoustic engine system disclosed in WO2010107308A1 by De Block et al. The multi-stage traveling wave thermoacoustic engine system is composed of a plurality of identical traveling wave thermoacoustic engines, and each traveling wave thermoacoustic engine is connected through a resonance tube to form a loop. This system can effectively recover the acoustic work dissipated in the resonance tube, and avoid the problem of excessive size of the resonance tube. Compared with the thermoacoustic engine system described in Figure 1, the structure is more compact and the potential effect is higher. However, the thermal buffer tube structure is not added to the thermoacoustic engine, resulting in the mixing loss of cold and hot gases, resulting in a large amount of heat loss in the resonance tube, and at the same time, there is no DC device installed in the loop system, so this structure has not been achieved. better effect. In particular, this system is not suitable for utilizing the exhaust heat of the flue gas, which is a heat source whose temperature gradually decreases.

CN103758657A. The traveling wave thermoacoustic power generation system is mainly composed of at least three acoustic resonance type traveling wave thermoacoustic engines, at least one linear motor and a resonance tube. Compared with the structure in Figure 2, this system adds a thermal buffer tube and a subcooler to the thermoacoustic engine unit, and adds a high-temperature end-laminar fluidized screen and a low-temperature end-laminar fluidized screen at both ends of the thermal buffer tube , which solves the problem of cold and heat loss in the thermoacoustic engine system; and, the system is installed with a loop DC suppressor to eliminate the system's DC; due to the changes in the above two points, the performance of the system has been significantly improved. 以上所述的行波热声发动机都是利用定温热源，其加热器一般是工作在固定的温度，难于高效梯级利用温度变化的烟气余热。The traveling wave thermoacoustic engines mentioned above all use a constant temperature heat source, and their heaters generally work at a fixed temperature, so it is difficult to efficiently utilize the waste heat of flue gas with temperature changes in steps.

为了解决传统系统利用烟气余热这种变温热源的高效转换以及简洁输出的问题，本发明提出了一种新型的流程，它可以高效低梯级利用不同温度品位的热源，同时输出采用单一输出，降低了系统复杂性。In order to solve the problem of high-efficiency conversion and simple output of the variable-temperature heat source using flue gas waste heat in the traditional system, this invention proposes a new type of process, which can utilize heat sources of different temperature grades efficiently and in low steps, and simultaneously output adopts a single output , reducing the system complexity.

It has a simple structure without moving parts, and the thermoacoustic engine unit works in the Gas waste heat, as the heat source at the hot end, can utilize heat energy of different temperature grades at the same time. The heat energy utilization efficiency is high, and it has broad development and application prospects.

the waste heat of such high-temperature flue gas passes through the heater of each stage of thermoacoustic engine unit in turn; the main cooler 2 and secondary cooler 8 of each stage of thermoacoustic engine unit are cooled by water coolers to maintain Room temperature range; a temperature gradient is formed on the regenerator 3 of each thermoacoustic engine unit; under the temperature gradient condition, a thermoacoustic effect is generated between the internal working gas of the regenerator 3 of each thermoacoustic engine unit and the solid filler therein , convert the waste heat of high-temperature flue gas input to the heater 4 of each stage of thermoacoustic engine unit into sound work, the sound work propagates along the positive direction of the temperature gradient, and the sound work passes through the thermal buffer tube of the first-stage thermoacoustic engine unit in turn 6 and the secondary heat exchanger 8, and then transmitted to the next-stage thermoacoustic engine unit through the resonant tube 9;
